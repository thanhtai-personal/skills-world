# Code of Conduct

Skill World welcomes constructive technical discussion and contributions. Treat contributors and upstream authors with respect.

Be specific about code, behavior, evidence, licenses, architecture, and trade-offs rather than attacking people. Harassment, threats, discriminatory abuse, doxxing, deliberate exposure of private information, and sustained bad-faith disruption are not acceptable.

Disagreement is normal. Challenge ideas with evidence, preserve attribution, and make room for maintainers to reject changes that do not fit the project's scope or quality bar.

For conduct concerns, contact the maintainer privately rather than escalating sensitive details in a public issue.
