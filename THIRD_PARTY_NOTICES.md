## Rule for future imports

Before merging a new third-party skill into Skill World:

1. identify the original author/owner and collection;
2. verify the upstream license for the exact skill/snapshot;
3. store a sibling `PROVENANCE.md`;
4. preserve or copy required license/NOTICE material;
5. record whether the local copy is verbatim or adapted;
6. add the source to this file if it introduces a new upstream collection/license; and
7. never assume the root Skill World MIT license overrides a third-party license.

This notice is a project-maintenance record, not legal advice. If a license has special redistribution conditions, those conditions control.
